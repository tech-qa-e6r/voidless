01 Live Preview

Markdown renders right in the text — move the caret into a line to reveal the raw syntax.

02 Split & Drag

Drag a tab to any edge to split the pane; drop it in the centre to just move it.

03 Wiki-Links

[[note]] links with autocomplete over the vault; the brackets stay hidden until you edit.

04 Tables

Markdown tables become editable tables in place, with per-column option chips.

05 Templates

Reusable note templates with variables like {{date}} — auto-applied per folder.

06 Alarms

Put a time in a note and get an in-app reminder with a chime; click it to jump back to the line.

07 Base View

Roll a folder of notes up into a sortable table or a kanban board, built from their frontmatter.

08 Excalidraw

Draw diagrams inside a note without leaving the editor.

09 Anki

A built-in spaced-repetition trainer — add a card from any word, translated by DeepL.

10 Web Parser

Give it sites and keywords; it crawls the pages and collects matching links into a table.

11 Google Drive

Sync the vault through your own Drive, with move and conflict detection.

12 Color & Icon

Colour-code files and folders with icons — the labels sync across devices.

13 Report

One click writes a diagnostic report into the vault's logs folder — handy when filing a bug.

14 Search

Full-text search across the whole vault, with results in context.
